# Velnox brand guidelines

## The mark
A V built from four interlaced bands. Every stroke sits on a 30° lattice, every band has the same width, every gap is the same width. Several paths, one shape: many sellers, one platform. The same mark is used at every size and in every file; there is no alternate or simplified version.

The name is set in wide, bold capitals, drawn as paths (not a font), so it renders identically everywhere.

## Colour

| Role | Name | Hex |
|---|---|---|
| Primary dark | Slate 900 | `#0F172A` |
| Dark background | Night | `#0B1220` |
| Brand accent | Emerald | `#10B981` |
| Text-safe green on light | Emerald dark | `#047857` |
| Mark on dark | Silver gradient | `#F6F8FA` → `#BCC4CC` → `#EEF1F3` → `#8E98A2` → `#D9DEE2` |
| Reversed | White | `#FFFFFF` |

- On white, use `#047857` for the name. Plain `#10B981` is for dark backgrounds, tiles and large graphics.
- On dark, `#10B981` on `#0B1220` reads clearly.

## Clear space
Keep clear space on every side of a lockup equal to the cap height of the "V" in VELNOX. For the mark alone, keep one quarter of the mark's height clear.

## Minimum size
| Asset | Minimum |
|---|---|
| Mark | 24 px wide on screen · 8 mm in print (in a 16 px favicon the fine lines merge into a soft V silhouette) |
| Wordmark | 72 px wide on screen · 20 mm in print |
| Horizontal lockup | 140 px wide |
| Stacked lockup | 96 px wide |

## Which version where
- Favicons and small icons: the same mark on a square tile (`favicon/`)
- Up to about 96 px wide: the mark alone
- Headers and documents: horizontal lockup
- Square spaces and signage: stacked lockup
- App stores and home screens: `png/app-icons/`

## Do not
- Do not recolour outside the colourways provided.
- Do not stretch, rotate, outline, add shadows, bevels or glows.
- Do not rebuild the wordmark in a font.
- Do not place the dark mark on dark photos or the silver mark on white.
- Do not separate or rearrange the strands of the mark.
- Do not put the logo on busy backgrounds without a solid or darkened panel behind it.

## Silver mark
The silver gradient is a finish for dark backgrounds. For web UI, buttons and print, use the flat colourways.
